## DiCo steering

The DiCo steering extension separates shared calculations (`steering.js`), read orchestration (`steering-service.js`) and browser rendering (`steering-client.js` / `steering-client.css`). Localized navigation keeps Mon temps, Projets, Unité DiCo and the fixed Programmes selector available independently of steering configuration. The older internal steering portfolio retains its unit and leader/contributor views and programme-assignment calculations. [Pilotage](PILOTAGE.md) documents that separate API, configuration, source restrictions and test harnesses.
